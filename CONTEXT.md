# Sabre Whereabouts

Where every trooper in the company is and will be (leave, off, medical, courses, RF/Extra/SOL), told to commanders on Telegram and fed into the parade state.

## Telegram

**Commanders chat**:
A platoon's (or COY HQ's) Telegram group, or one topic in it, linked with `/here`, where its commanders get cards and digests.
_Avoid_: group, platoon chat, topic

**Card**:
A message about one record, outcome or RF request that is remembered and redrawn in every copy whenever that thing changes.
_Avoid_: notification, post, alert

**Own copy**:
The raiser's private copy of an RF request's card, which offers Close and nothing else.
_Avoid_: DM copy

**Audience**:
Who hears about something: a platoon's commanders chat, one man, or the admins.
_Avoid_: recipient, target

**Courier**:
The one place whereabouts speaks on Telegram on its own initiative, by audience or by card; replies to a tap are not the Courier's.
_Avoid_: notifier, outbound, sender

## Who may

**Grant**:
A role a member holds over the company, one platoon or one camp. A GC's grant names his camp; without one it counts for nothing.
_Avoid_: permission, rank

**Access**:
What one member may do now with one record, RF request, post or man, and which screens he sees, worked out from his grants and the thing's state.
_Avoid_: permission, rights, can

**Reach**:
How far a member's grants carry for one kind of access: the whole company, some platoons and camps, or nothing.
_Avoid_: scope, coverage

## Writes

**Write**:
One change to whereabouts by a member or the system: refused whole, or committed with its audit row, then the parade state brought in line and the Courier told.
_Avoid_: mutation, transaction, action

**Refusal**:
Why a write didn't happen, in words for whoever tried.
_Avoid_: error, rejection (a rejection is a commander's decision on a request)
